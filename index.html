About

I am pursuing an integrated M.Sc.–M.Tech. in Mathematics and Data & Computational Science at the Indian Institute of Technology, Jodhpur.

My work sits where applied mathematics meets deep learning. I have worked on tumour segmentation for histopathology slides, variational and level-set methods such as Chan-Vese, and image denoising. I also work on generative AI and agentic AI for medical images, including generative models for CT slices and a multimodal AI dermatology assistant that combines image analysis, voice and LLM reasoning.
